# Chog Stack — visual direction

**Metaphor: a construction site drawn on Chog's street wall.** The tower is a building in progress, so the
screen reads like an architect's elevation drawing done in chalk and marker: a ruler with
`+12.4 m` level marks, dimension lines, hazard tape and a sketched tower crane. The Chog art
is the only full colour; everything else is ink on warm paper.

References (principles taken, not copied):
- https://chog.xyz — sponsor identity: Koulen display caps, Gaegu handwriting, warm paper wall
  (#D8D6C7), pink pill buttons (#CE437C), sketchy street art.
- https://chogathon.chog.xyz — the purple Monad accent on dark tiles.
- Tower Bloxx / Stack-style casual games — a single big score number, one-tap action, camera
  that follows the top.

Tokens
- Type: Koulen (display caps, height meter), Gaegu (chalk annotations, level marks, callouts),
  DM Sans (UI text).
- Paper #EFEBDF, wall #D8D6C7, ink #17141A, Chog pink #D83E7E, Monad purple #836EF9,
  crown yellow #FFC93C, mint #2FCB98.
- Buttons: pill, 2.5px ink border, 0 4px 0 ink lip; pressed state drops the lip.
- Pieces: 5px ink outline around the trait-built shape, filled with the real Chog art.
